OEMK / Phase #0

亻象

Elephant Tale

In Chinese, 象 carries echoes of elephant, image, and phenomenon.

像 adds the person radical 亻, becoming likeness, figure, or idol.

亻象 fractures the image back open.

Elephant Tale moves between elephant, image, idol, and the illegible body as it appears through fragments, proxies, and replicas.

The OEMK project is supported by the Kone Foundation.
