Ginko Hsu (Jingke Xu) · Helsinki, Finland

I am Ginko Hsu, a Helsinki-based visual artist. My work moves between memory and myth, exploring forms of personal and collective memory that exist outside established archives. Working across sculpture, glass, ceramics, imagery, publication, poetry, moving image, and installation, my practice embraces materials rich with contradiction and hybridity: glass and ceramics that fracture yet endure, and words that knot past to present.

I am currently developing Olla ei mistään kotoisin (Of No Origin) (OEMK), a long-term, research-based project involving exhibitions, publications, and field research. It explores absence and illegibility as pathways to other forms of knowledge. OEMK grows partly from my experience as a Chinese immigrant living between places and languages, and from family histories that have reached me only in fragments: histories that were only partially inherited, remain inaccessible, or are difficult to speak about.

I hold an MFA from the Academy of Fine Arts, University of the Arts Helsinki. My practice has been supported by the Kone Foundation, the Academy of Fine Arts Foundation, and Greta och Alfred Runebergs stiftelse. Alongside my artistic practice, I am the co-founder and curator of Kuu-si, an artist-run, non-profit exhibition space in Helsinki.
